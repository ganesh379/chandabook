ChandaBook — Privacy Policy

Google Play Production Policy Compliant

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Overview & Purpose

ChandaBook is designed for community festival committees, mandals, and devotional trusts to maintain transparent financial accounting of festival collections (Chanda), expenditures, pledges, and devotee prasadam sponsorships.

2. Information We Collect

  • Donor Information: Name, phone number (used strictly to dispatch digital receipts via WhatsApp upon user action), donation amount, and payment mode.
  • Financial Records: Categorized festival expenditures, payment receipts, and volunteer collector tags.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to notify committee members of newly recorded donations or expenses.

3. Data Usage & Security

All festival records are stored with local offline persistence and securely synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore over encrypted SSL/TLS connections. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your donor data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. User Rights & Account / Data Deletion Request

Festival committees and volunteers retain full ownership of their data. You can delete or request full erasure of your account and all associated festival records at any time through either of the following methods:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the ChandaBook app → Go to Settings & Reports → Tap "Delete Group / Clear Data" to instantly erase your local data and cached transactions.
  • Web / Email Deletion Request: If you wish to permanently delete your festival group, cloud records, or registered email from our cloud database, send an email to ganeshpudi12@gmail.com with your Festival Group Name or 6-digit code. All associated records will be permanently purged within 48 hours.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at ganeshpudi12@gmail.com.
